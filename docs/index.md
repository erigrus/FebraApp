# Febra

A local-only fever tracker for iPhone and iPad, in English and German.

Enter each measurement by hand, see the history as a chart with age-dependent
fever thresholds and a trend, log medication doses with their minimum interval,
and export a history as a PDF for the doctor.

**Everything stays on your device.** No account, no sign-in, no cloud, no
analytics — the app makes no network requests at all.

- [Privacy policy](privacy-policy) · [Datenschutzerklärung](privacy-policy.de)
- [Support](support)

Febra is no substitute for medical advice. With a high or persistent fever,
please consult a doctor.
